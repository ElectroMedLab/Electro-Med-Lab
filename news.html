News and highlights

Milestones, publications and updates from the lab.

2026

Plenary Lecture at the World Congress on Nanomedicine

Dr Akhil Jain has been invited to deliver a Plenary Lecture at the World Congress on Nanomedicine 2026 in New Delhi, chaired by Nobel Laureate Prof. Roger D. Kornberg.

2026

EPSRC Open Fellowship awarded

Dr Jain was awarded an EPSRC Open Fellowship to establish the Electro-Med group. The funding covers five years, from 2026 to 2031, and the lab will be recruiting postdoctoral researchers every year over that period.

2026

New publication in the Journal of Physics: Photonics

Our paper, “Quantum sensing of biomolecules: principles, progress and prospects”, has been published in the Journal of Physics: Photonics. Read the paper →

2025

New publication in the Journal of Materials Chemistry C

Our collaborative paper with Dr Eva Hemmer's group at the University of Ottawa, “Rapid microwave-assisted synthesis of morphology-controlled luminescent lanthanide-doped Gd2O2S nanostructures”, has been published in the Journal of Materials Chemistry C. Read the paper →

2026

Funding awarded for a brand new fluorescence microscope

We have been awarded funding for a brand new fluorescence microscope. The Zeiss Axiovert 7 will support DAPI, GFP and Cy5 live and fixed cell imaging with multiposition time lapse capability for the group.
